Why this symposium

Our dialysis patients are getting older, more often multimorbid and have frequently undergone multiple vascular procedures. There is no longer a single standard approach to vascular access that fits every situation.

Sustainable solutions come from collaboration between disciplines. That is why the Cologne symposium focuses on real cases and open discussion.
